GHANA CUSTOMS ESTIMATE

Estimate your import costs.

Use this planning calculator to estimate customs duty, NHIL, GETFund Levy and VAT on an import. The final amount is determined through the official customs assessment.

GRA uses five import-duty bands: 0%, 5%, 10%, 20% and 35%. For this planning estimate, VAT is 15% and NHIL and GETFund Levy are 2.5% each. The tax base is calculated from the customs/CIF value plus the selected import duty and other applicable import taxes entered above. Actual assessment can vary by HS classification, exemptions and other applicable charges.
